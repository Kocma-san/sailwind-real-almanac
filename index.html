Sailwind Real Almanac

Line of position - Desmos Realistic Skies

Guide

  1. Enter current date / time
  2. Enter best dead reckoning (DR)
  3. Choose 2 stars (~90° apart) or 3 stars (~120° apart), heights ~15–65°
  4. Take a fix with the quintant for each star and adjust time or reading
  5. Open Desmos
    • Enter DR lat / long
    • For each reading:
      • Observed altitude of the target — Sight line 1
      • Aestrin Hour Angle of Aries — Sight line 2
        (from this page, not the in-game New-sky almanac)
      • Sidereal Hour Angle of the target — Sight line 3
      • Declination of the Target — Sight line 4
  6. Your estimated position is on Desmos lines 75–76

Use stars with this tool (not planets). Planet tables in the in-game almanac are for the mod’s solar system / New sky workflow; this page only has fixed star SHA/Dec for the Real skybox.
