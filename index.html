Guide

  1. Enter current date / time
  2. Enter best dead reckoning (DR)
  3. Choose 2 stars (~90° apart) or 3 stars (~120° apart), heights ~15–65°.
    Tip: open Stellarium Web with your DR lat/lon and the date/time shown below for a sky preview and to pick stars.
  4. Take a fix with the quintant for each star and adjust day / time or reading (each sight has its own day for midnight crossings)
  5. The graph on the right syncs DR + sight values automatically.
    • Drag the triangle point (a, b) onto the LOP intersection
    • Estimated position appears under the graph (and Desmos lines 75–76)

Use stars with this tool (not planets). Planet tables in the in-game almanac are for the mod’s solar system / New sky workflow; this page only has fixed star SHA/Dec for the Real skybox.

The Desmos graph needs your own free API key (section 1). Create one at desmos.com/my-api — it stays in your browser only.
